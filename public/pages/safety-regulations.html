Safety & Regulations

Our commitment to a secure and spiritual experience

📍 The Starting Point

The journey officially commences from Yogpeeth BACE. We have arranged abundant, high-quality space for all students to stay comfortably before the bus departs. Separate, secure wings are designated for male and female students to ensure privacy and comfort.

🏨 Accommodation in Vrindavan

Student Quarters

Students will be accommodated in clean, ashram-style guesthouses near the Krishna Balaram Temple.

Supervision

Mentors will be staying in the same facility to provide 24/7 assistance and guidance.

📜 General Guidelines

  • Punctuality: Students must adhere strictly to the bus departure and session timings.
  • Identification: Always carry your student ID card and a copy of the registration receipt.
  • Dress Code: Please wear modest, comfortable clothing suitable for temple visits. Carry warm layers for the night.
  • No-Solo Wandering: For safety, students are not permitted to leave the group or the accommodation area without informing a mentor.

Your safety is our priority so that you can focus on the divinity.
