Holistic Wellness

The Art of Connection

Yoga is not just a physical exercise; it is a ladder to the Divine. At Yogpeeth, we blend the structural discipline of Ashtanga Yoga with the profound spiritual depth of Mantra Meditation.

The 8 Limbs (Ashtanga)

🧘‍♂️

Asana

Physical postures to purify the body and prepare the mind for stillness.

💨

Pranayama

Breath control to harmonize the vital energies within the subtle body.

🧠

Dharana

Concentration techniques to fix the wandering mind on a single point.

✨

Samadhi

The ultimate stage of union where the soul connects with the Supreme.

The Heart of Yoga: Mantra

While physical yoga prepares the temple of the body, Mantra Meditation (Sound Vibration) awakens the soul. In this age, the chanting of sacred sound is the most effective means for peace and realization.

MAHA MANTRA

Hare Krishna Hare Krishna
Krishna Krishna Hare Hare
Hare Rama Hare Rama
Rama Rama Hare Hare

Chant and be Happy!
