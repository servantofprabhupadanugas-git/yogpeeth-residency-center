Missionary & Behavioral Rules

These rules apply to all virtual members and well-wishers of the Yogpeeth mission:

  • ✔

    Active Participation: Members are encouraged to enthusiastically participate in the mission of distributing Vedic wisdom through various digital programs and outreach services.

  • ✘

    Zero Obstruction: Voluntary obstruction, negative propaganda, or any activity that hinders the ongoing social and spiritual services of Yogpeeth is strictly discouraged.

  • ✔

    Digital Integrity: Use Krishna's digital property and platforms solely for the purpose of preaching and community building, not for personal or commercial gain.

  • ✔

    Harmonious Speech: All communication in virtual groups or forums must be rooted in respect, love, and trust, avoiding frivolous talk.

🏠

Residential Regulations

Hare Krishna! Dear Student,

To ensure a balanced life between academics and spiritual growth, residents must adhere to:

Daily Sadhana

  • Attend morning program in time (Annexure I).
  • Follow "Early to bed, early to rise" discipline.
  • Fill Sadhana cards regularly for the facilitator.
  • Wear Dhoti-Kurta for internal programs.

The 4 Regulative Principles

  • No Meat Eating & No Intoxication.
  • No Illicit Sex & No Gambling.
  • Avoid tea, coffee, and outside junk food.
  • No movies or frivolous worldly music.

General Conduct:

Residents should not bring newspapers/magazines, must speak in English as much as possible, and must inform authorities of their whereabouts at all times. Money exchanges between devotees and bringing outside friends for overnight stays are strictly prohibited.
