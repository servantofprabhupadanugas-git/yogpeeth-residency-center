De-addicting by Re-addicting

The Bhagavad Gita teaches that we cannot simply "stop" a habit; we must replace it. By attaching our minds to higher spiritual tastes, the lower material intoxications naturally lose their grip.

विषया विनिवर्तन्ते निराहारस्य देहिनः ।
रसवर्जं रसोऽप्यस्य परं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तते ॥

"The embodied soul may be restricted from sense enjoyment, though the taste for sense objects remains. But, ceasing such engagements by experiencing a higher taste, he is fixed in consciousness." — Gita 2.59
