What is Daily Darshan?

1. Divine Vision

In spiritual terms, Darshan means "seeing." Beholding the beautiful form of the Deity in the temple is a meditative act that purifies the heart and connects us to the Divine.

2. Philosophy

Darshan also refers to Philosophy (Tattva). It is the lens through which we understand the Absolute Truth. We provide daily philosophical glances through the words of Srila Prabhupada.

Today's Wisdom
